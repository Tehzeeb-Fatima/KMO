1. First time login

KMO team aap ka account banate hain aur aap ko 1 email bhejte hain. Us email ke link se aap apna password khud set karte hain.

Step 1

Email se password set karein

  1. Apni Gmail open karein. Karachi Mart Online ki email search karein.
  2. Email mein button par tap karein. 1 page open hoga.
  3. New password 2 dafa type karein aur save karein. Aap direct apne dashboard mein pohanch jayenge.
Email nahi mili?Gmail mein Spam aur Promotions folder check karein. Link 24 hours tak valid hai. Expire ho jaye to KMO team ko call karein, woh new link bhej denge.

Step 2

Next time login

  1. Phone ke browser (Chrome) mein vendor.karachimartonline.com open karein.
  2. Email aur Password type karein.
  3. Sign in par tap karein.
Password bhool gaye?Forgot password? par tap karein aur apni email type karein. New password set karne ka link email par aa jayega.
Easy tipChrome mein page open karke ⋮ menu se Add to Home screen kar lein. Phir dashboard app ki tarah 1 tap se open hoga.

2. Dashboard aur menu

Login ke baad sab se pehle Overview open hota hai. Yahan is month ki sale aur new orders show hote hain.

Overview

Aap ke store ki summary

  • Sales this month: is month kitne Rupees ki sale hui.
  • Pending orders: woh orders jin par abhi aap ko kaam karna hai.
  • Recent orders: new orders ki list. Kisi order par tap karein to us ki details open hongi.

Menu

☰ button se har page par jayein

Upar left side par 3 lines wala ☰ button par tap karein. Menu open ho jayega. Sab se important pages:

  • Products: product add aur edit karna
  • Orders: customer ke orders
  • Payments: aap ki payments
  • Store settings: store ka name, logo aur banner

Neeche Sign out se logout kar sakte hain.

3. Naya product kaise add karein

Product ka form 4 parts mein hai. Upar se neeche fill karte jayein aur end mein 1 dafa Save product par tap karein. Bas.

Step 1

Products open karein aur "+ Add product" par tap karein

  1. Menu ☰ se Products open karein.
  2. Upar wale bare orange button + Add product par tap karein.

Isi page par aap ke saare products ki list hoti hai. Har product ke saath Edit aur Delete hai.

Step 2 · Product details

Name, category aur price

  1. Product name: product ka clear aur simple name likhein, jaise "Printed Cotton Kurti".
  2. Category: jo button aap ke product se match karta ho, us par 1 tap karein. Woh purple ho jayega aur neeche Selected likha aa jayega.
  3. Selling price: jis price par sell karna hai (sirf number, jaise 2200).
  4. Original price: sirf tab likhein jab sale lagani ho. Website par yeh price cut (line ke saath) show hoti hai. Optional hai.
  5. Description: fabric, size details, aur box mein kya kya hai.
Sub-category zaroori haiSirf main category (jaise "Kids") select nahi hoti. Uske andar wali sub-category select karein, jaise "Girls Clothing". Aap ki category ya poora group show nahi ho raha to KMO team ko batayein, woh add kar denge.

Step 3 · Photos

Photos lagayein

  1. + Add photos wale box par tap karein.
  2. Gallery se 1 ya zyada photos select karein, ya camera se new photo lein.
  3. Pehli photo Main hoti hai. Customer sab se pehle wohi dekhta hai. Kisi aur photo ko main banana ho to us par Make main tap karein.
  4. Galat photo remove karni ho to corner wala × tap karein.
Achhi photo ke liyeDaylight mein, plain white wall ya bedsheet ke saamne photo lein. Product poora show ho. Kam se kam 1 photo zaroori hai.

Step 4 · Sizes & colours

Size ya colour (agar hon)

Agar product different sizes ya colours mein hai, to har 1 alag se add karein:

  1. Size, Colour ya Other mein se select karein.
  2. Pehle box mein size/colour type karein, jaise Medium ya Red.
  3. Quantity mein type karein ke yeh wala aap ke paas kitne pieces hain.
  4. + Add par tap karein. Yeh upar list mein aa jayega. Next size isi tarah add karein.

Agar product 1 hi tarah ka hai (koi size ya colour nahi), to yeh part skip kar dein.

Step 5 · Stock aur save

Stock check karein aur Save karein

  • Stock: agar aap ne sizes daale hain to yeh automatic total ho jata hai (5 + 8 + 4 = 17). Sizes nahi daale to yahan total quantity likhein.
  • More details: brand, weight wagera. Optional hai, skip kar sakte hain.
  • Send for approval: isay ON (orange) rehne dein.

End mein Save product par tap karein aur photos upload hone tak wait karein.

Kuch miss ho gaya?Agar name, category, price ya photo reh gayi ho to Save ke upar red colour mein message aayega ke kya fill karna hai. Woh fill karke dobara Save karein.

Step 6 · Approval

Product "Pending" mein chala jata hai

Save ke baad product list mein Pending likha aata hai. Iska matlab hai KMO team usay check kar rahi hai. Approve hote hi woh Live ho jata hai aur website par customers ko show hone lagta hai.

Aap ko kuch nahi karna, bas wait karein. Approval ki notification bell icon 🔔 mein aa jati hai.

4. Product edit karein

Price badalni ho, stock khatam ho gaya ho, ya nayi photo lagani ho: sab Products page se hota hai.

  • Price ya stock badalna: product ke saath Edit par tap karein, number change karein, phir Save product.
  • 1 size khatam ho gaya: Edit mein us size ke saath Remove tap karein, ya us ki sahi quantity dobara add karein.
  • Product remove karna: Delete par tap karein. Confirm karne ke liye 1 dafa aur poocha jayega.
  • Product search karna: upar Search your products mein name type karein, ya category select karein.

5. Order aaye to kya karein

Naya order aate hi bell icon 🔔 par notification aati hai aur order Orders page par Pending mein show hota hai.

Step 1

Orders page open karein

  1. Menu ☰ se Orders open karein.
  2. Upar Pending par tap karein to sirf new orders show honge.
  3. Order number (jaise #KMO-…) par tap karein.

Step 2

Order check karein aur status update karein

Items mein likha hota hai ke kya bhejna hai, kaun sa size aur kitne pieces.

Har step par neeche list se next status select karein aur Update status par tap karein:

  1. Confirm order: order check kar liya, stock available hai.
  2. Mark as processing: pack kar rahe hain.
  3. Mark as ready to ship: parcel ready hai.
  4. Mark as shipped: rider/courier ko de diya.
  5. Mark as delivered: customer ko mil gaya.
Kyun zaroori hai?Har update KMO team ko show hota hai, aur customer apne account mein order ka status dekhta hai. Status time par update karne se delivery late nahi hoti aur customer satisfied rehta hai.
Stock khatam hai?Agar order complete nahi ho sakta to Cancel order par tap karein, lekin pehle KMO team ko inform kar dein.

6. Courier aur delivery

KMO ke orders Courier Fast Service se jate hain. Parcel aap ke ghar ya shop se pickup hota hai aur customer tak pohanchta hai.

Step 1: Courier account connect karein (sirf 1 dafa)

  1. KMO team aap ka Courier Fast Service account banate hain. Is account ki email wahi hoti hai jo aap ki KMO login email hai.
  2. Account ready hone par vendor portal kholte hi Connect your Courier account ka popup aata hai.
  3. KMO team ka diya hua courier password likhein aur Connect par tap karein.
  4. Connected likha aa jaye to kaam ho gaya. Abhi time nahi to Later dabayein, popup agli dafa phir aayega.
Password ka error aa raha hai?KMO team ko call karein. Woh naya password set kar denge.

Step 2: Har order ki courier booking

Booking 2 tareeqon se ho sakti hai:

  1. KMO portal se (asaan tareeqa): Orders page par order kholein aur Request courier booking dabayein. Ek saath kai orders bhejne hon to list mein tick karke upar wala button dabayein. KMO team check karke booking kar dete hain.
  2. Courier ki website se: Fast Service ke portal par usi email se login karein aur khud booking karein.

Step 3: Packing aur pickup

  • Product achhi tarah pack karein taake raste mein kharab na ho.
  • Product ka weight sahi likhein (product form mein "Weight in grams"). Courier charges weight se lagte hain.
  • Fast Service ka rider aap se parcel pickup karta hai aur customer ke address par deliver karta hai.
  • Har step par order ka status update karein (chapter 5), jaise Ready to ship, Shipped.

Customer se delivery charges

  1. Menu ☰ se Shipping open karein.
  2. Your delivery charge to customers mein city select karein, fee (Rs.) likhein aur Save dabayein.
  3. Har city ka alag charge rakh sakte hain. Other select karke baaki sab cities ka ek charge rakhein.
  4. Aap kisi city ka charge set na karein to KMO ka default charge lagta hai.
Customer ko pehle se pata hota haiDelivery charge checkout par order confirm hone se pehle customer ko dikhta hai, aur customer se hi liya jata hai.

Delivery na ho sake ya parcel wapas aaye

  • Customer ghar par na ho to courier 2 dafa dobara delivery try karta hai.
  • Customer parcel wapas kare to return saboot (evidence) ke saath complete hota hai, taake record saaf rahe.
  • Returned, cancelled ya undelivered order ki payment nahi banti.

7. Apna store setup karein

Logo aur banner wala store customers ko zyada professional aur trusted lagta hai. Yeh kaam sirf 1 dafa karna hai.

  1. Menu ☰ se Store settings open karein.
  2. + Logo: apne store ka logo lagayein (square photo behtar hai).
  3. + Upload banner: 1 wide photo, jaise apne products ki.
  4. Location mein apna area likhein, jaise Gulshan-e-Iqbal.
  5. Store description mein 2 lines likhein ke aap kya bechte hain.
  6. Sab se neeche Save settings par tap karein.

Vacation mode: holiday par ja rahe hon to isay ON karke Save karein. Aap ke store page par holiday ka notice lag jata hai. Long holiday ho to KMO team ko bhi bata dein.

8. Payments

Customer courier rider ko cash (COD) deta hai. Courier woh amount KMO ko bhejta hai, aur KMO delivered orders ki payment aap ko deta hai.

  • Available balance: jo amount aap withdraw kar sakte hain.
  • Paid to date: ab tak aap ko kitni payment mil chuki hai.
  • Request a withdrawal: amount likhein (khaali chhor dein to full balance) aur Send request to admin par tap karein. KMO team payment bhej denge.
Invoice aur payment kab?Hafte mein 1 ya 2 invoice ban sakti hain, jaisa aap ko suit kare. Invoice banne ke agle din payment ho jati hai. Sirf delivered orders ki payment banti hai.
Commission aur membershipPehle 3 mahine KMO koi commission nahi leta. Uske baad delivered orders par 8% commission. Pehle 2 mahine membership free, phir Rs. 499 monthly. Details isi page par Read the full vendor agreement mein hain.

9. Bulk upload: ek saath bahut se products

Bahut se products hon to ek ek add karne ki zaroorat nahi. Excel jaisi file se sab ek saath upload karein.

  1. Products page par Download reference file dabayein. Is file mein 1 sample row hai jo format dikhati hai.
  2. File Excel ya Google Sheets mein kholein aur har row mein 1 product likhein: name, category, price, description waghera.
  3. Category wahi likhein jo file mein di gayi list mein hai (sub-category).
  4. File CSV format mein save karein aur Bulk upload dabakar select karein.
  5. Upload ke baad har product mein photos lagayein aur approval ke liye bhejein.
Error aaya?Message batata hai ke kis row mein kya ghalat hai, jaise category match nahi hui. Theek karke file dobara upload karein.

Ek saath delete: products list mein tick karein aur Delete selected dabayein. Products archive ho jate hain aur customers ko nahi dikhte.

10. Messages aur notifications

Customers aap ke store page se aap ko message kar sakte hain.

  • Menu ☰ se Messages kholein. Customers ke messages aur KMO Support ki chat yahin hoti hai.
  • Customer ko jaldi jawab dein. Jaldi reply se sale hone ke chances barhte hain.
  • Upar ghanti (bell) mein notifications aati hain: naya order, product approve ya reject, payment aur messages.
  • Customer ka number ya details apni marketing ke liye use na karein. Yeh KMO policy ke khilaf hai.

11. Product rules: jaldi approval ke liye

In baaton ka khayal rakhein to product jaldi approve hota hai aur customers ko achha lagta hai.

  • Pehli photo sirf product ki: saaf background par product. Model wali photo baad mein lagayein.
  • Sub-category zaroori: sirf main category nahi chalegi.
  • Colours aur sizes: sirf woh colours likhein jo aap ke paas hain. Har colour ki photo us colour ke saath lagayein, taake customer jo colour chune wahi photo dikhe.
  • Name aur description: saaf naam likhein, jaise "Rotating Spice Rack with 12 Jars". Description chhoti aur kaam ki rakhein: material, size, kya saath milta hai.
  • Emoji na lagayein: name aur description mein emoji save nahi hote, khud hat jate hain.
  • Ek product ek hi dafa: same product dobara upload na karein. Naya colour ho to usi product mein colour add karein.
  • Optional: Urdu description aur product video bhi laga sakte hain.
  • Stock aur price: stock khatam ho to foran update karein, aur price final rakhein.

FAQs: common questions

Mera product website par show kyun nahi ho raha?

Products page par us ka status check karein. Pending ho to KMO team ki approval ka wait hai. Draft ho to Edit karke "Send for approval" ON karein aur Save karein.

Photo upload nahi ho rahi.

JPG, PNG ya WEBP photo select karein. Internet slow ho to Save ke baad page close na karein, upload complete hone dein.

Save kiya, red colour mein message aa gaya.

Woh batata hai ke kya miss hua hai: name, category, price ya kam se kam 1 photo. Woh fill karke dobara Save karein.

Mujhe apni category nahi mil rahi.

Main category ke andar wali sub-category select karein. Agar aap ka poora group show nahi ho raha (jaise aap jewellery bhi bechte hain), to KMO team ko call karein. Woh aap ke store mein add kar denge.

Parcel kis courier se jayega?

Courier Fast Service se. Pehli dafa popup mein courier account connect karein, phir har order par "Request courier booking" dabayein. Details chapter 6 mein hain.

Customer ghar par nahi tha, ab kya hoga?

Courier 2 dafa dobara delivery try karta hai. Phir bhi na ho to parcel aap ko wapas aata hai aur us order ki payment nahi banti.

Payment kab milegi?

Hafte mein 1 ya 2 invoice banti hain. Invoice ke agle din delivered orders ki payment aap ko mil jati hai.

Phone par menu nahi mil raha.

Upar left corner mein ☰ (3 lines) wala button tap karein.

Kya computer se bhi chala sakte hain?

Ji haan. Computer par bhi vendor.karachimartonline.com open karein. Wahan menu hamesha left side par khula rehta hai.
